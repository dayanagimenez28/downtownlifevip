What wonderful news! **Starting November 26, 2026, Copa Airlines flies direct from Panama City to Porlamar, on Margarita Island, Venezuela.** There are three flights a week, on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Sundays, and tickets are already on sale at copaair.com.

For the island, this is much more than a new route. It is a door opening to the world. For years, getting to Margarita from abroad almost always meant flying into Caracas and then catching a domestic flight or the ferry. Now, with a single stop in Panama, you can reach the island the same day from dozens of cities across the Americas.

The timing matters too. After the June earthquakes, Venezuela went through some difficult months. Maiquetía, the main airport near Caracas, had no commercial flights for more than two months and only reopened in September, still using temporary terminals. That is why a direct international link that doesn't depend on a single airport is such good news for Margarita and for everyone who wants to visit.

## Why this flight is big news for Margarita Island

Panama City is Copa's hub, with more than 80 destinations across the continent. Suddenly, Margarita is a lot closer to Bogotá, Mexico City, Miami, Lima, Santo Domingo and many other cities.

Here is what I hope it brings:

- **Families reunited.** For Venezuelans living abroad, coming home for Christmas or the holidays becomes much easier.
- **Travelers discovering the island for the first time.** Margarita has beaches, history, great food and warm people. It is about time more visitors got to know it.
- **More life on the island.** More visitors means more business for local restaurants, guesthouses, guides and artisans.

## Copa Panama–Margarita Island flight schedule

| Flight | Route | Days | Departs | Arrives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CM651 | Panama City → Porlamar (Margarita) | Tue, Thu and Sun | 9:50 a.m. | 1:36 p.m. |
| CM652 | Porlamar (Margarita) → Panama City | Tue, Thu and Sun | 2:46 p.m. | 4:30 p.m. |

The flight takes about **2 hours and 45 minutes** and lands at **Santiago Mariño International Airport**, in the south of the island, right next to El Yaque beach. On booking sites you will see it listed as **Porlamar (PMV)**, the airport code for Margarita. Since you land around midday, you have time to drop off your bags and catch your first Margarita sunset that same evening.

Before booking, double-check the schedule on Copa's website, since new routes sometimes change. Also check Venezuela's entry requirements for your passport.

## How much does the flight from Panama to Margarita cost?

When I wrote this article, Copa was showing **round-trip economy fares from about 365 US dollars** between Panama City and Porlamar. Treat that as a guide: prices change with the date, how early you book and the season, and Christmas and Easter are usually more expensive. If you are flying from another city, the final fare depends on your connection in Panama.

My tip: if you already know your dates, book early, especially for the end-of-year holidays, when many Venezuelans fly home.

## From Margarita's airport to Pampatar

The drive from the airport to **Pampatar** takes about 30–40 minutes. It is a calm, pretty town with a colonial fort, a beach and seafront restaurants, and it sits right in the middle of the island, which makes it an easy base for exploring. ## Frequently asked questions

### Does Copa Airlines fly direct to Margarita Island?

Yes. Starting November 26, 2026, Copa flies direct from Panama City to Porlamar, on Margarita Island, three times a week.

### Which days does Copa fly from Panama to Margarita?

Tuesdays, Thursdays and Sundays. The flight leaves Panama at 9:50 a.m. and lands in Margarita at 1:36 p.m. local time.

### How much is a flight from Panama to Margarita Island?

When I wrote this article, Copa was showing round-trip economy fares from about 365 US dollars. Prices vary by date and season, so check copaair.com for current fares.

### How long is the flight from Panama to Margarita?

About 2 hours and 45 minutes.

### Are Porlamar and Margarita Island the same?

Porlamar is the largest city on Margarita Island, and airlines use its name for the island's airport (code PMV). If you are searching for flights to Porlamar, you are searching for flights to Margarita Island.

### Which airport does the flight land at?

Santiago Mariño International Airport, in the south of the island, next to El Yaque. Pampatar is about 30–40 minutes away by car.
